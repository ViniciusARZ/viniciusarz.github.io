Cultural Fluency Economic Model

The goal was to translate B-CFQ, a proprietary metric for measuring brand cultural fluency, into purchase intent and commercial impact.

FocusB-CFQ and purchase-intent association
ResultAdopted as a foundation of Collage Group's 2026 sales and marketing strategy
Problem

A proprietary brand measurement metric did not have a clear path to economic opportunity.

Collage Group already had a strong proprietary metric for measuring brand cultural fluency, however, the business struggled to connect it to revenue to attract new clients or show impact in existing accounts.

Solution

A slope model connecting cultural fluency to purchase intent.

Designed a method that estimates how movement in B-CFQ is associated with movement in purchase intent across industries. The model extracts a slope that can serve as an input to confidential client- and audience-specific projected calculations, so business opportunities can be measured by how cultural fluency improves.

01

Product-aligned measurement

The method stayed aligned with the B-CFQ metric used in the product, giving teams a consistent basis for interpretation.

02

Audience-specific projection

The slope made the association interpretable for a relevant audience or segment and usable in confidential projected-upside models.

Commercial translation

Sales and Client Services can use the slope as an input to client-specific economic models for projected upside. The conversion remains confidential.

Value

Adopted into Collage Group's 2026 sales and marketing strategy.

The rollout made B-CFQ commercially legible for new-business and lead-generation conversations. The analysis, expanded into a business model by the CEO, turned into the main sales and marketing strategy in 2026.
